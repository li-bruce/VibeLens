# Confirmed Business Rules

Source: simulated business-user answer `U002`, played by the coordinating AI and received as an actual forwarded message. Its exact text is retained in `user-answer-002.txt`. This is not independent human feedback or usability evidence. See `PROVENANCE-CORRECTION.md`.

- At upload, choose exactly one other existing user as reviewer. Self review is forbidden.
- The assigned reviewer reviews the file. Approval permits sharing. A non-approved review ends the file workflow; resubmission is out of scope.
- Only the uploader can share an approved file, with one designated recipient. Recipients cannot forward.
- File access consists of metadata and download. Every access requires login.
- The uploader and assigned reviewer may access before and after review.
- A recipient may access only after approval and an actual share to that account. All other users are denied.
- Use fixed test accounts. Registration and account administration are out of scope.
- Use Python, SQLite, and declaration-driven execution in this independent project.
